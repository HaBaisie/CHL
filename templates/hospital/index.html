About CHL Health Care Services

Serving Lamodi Community Since 2010

CHL Health Care Services is a premier healthcare facility located in the heart of Lamodi, Offa, Kwara State. Established in 2010, we have been at the forefront of providing quality, accessible, and affordable healthcare to the local community and surrounding areas.

Our mission is to deliver comprehensive healthcare services with compassion, respect, and excellence. We believe in treating the whole person, not just the illness, and we're committed to improving the health and wellbeing of every patient we serve.

Our Core Values:
  • Patient-Centered Care: We prioritize the needs and preferences of our patients in all decisions.
  • Medical Excellence: Our team of healthcare professionals maintains the highest standards of medical practice.
  • Community Focus: We are deeply committed to serving the Lamodi community and improving public health.
  • Innovation: We continuously adopt new technologies and methods to enhance patient care.
  • Accessibility: We strive to make quality healthcare accessible and affordable for all.
